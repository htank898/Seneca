## A. Birthday paradox (`birthday-paradox/birthday-paradox.mp4`)

Put 23 people in a meeting. What are the odds that two of them share a birthday?

Most people guess low. The answer is 50.7%.

At 30 people it's 70.6%. In a 70-person all-hands, it's 99.9%.

Our intuition fails because we picture *me* matching someone. But every pair in the room is a chance for a match, and 23 people make 253 pairs.

## B. 1% better (`one-percent/one-percent.mp4`)

"Get 1% better every day" is on every other post here. So I wanted to see the actual math.

1.01³⁶⁵ ≈ 37.8
0.99³⁶⁵ ≈ 0.03

That's a 2% daily difference, and a year later the two are about 1,481× apart.

After a month you are only at 1.35 vs 0.74. The gap is boring right up until it isn't.

I made this with Claude and Seneca's math skill. The animation is code (Manim), and every number on screen is computed rather than typed in by hand.
