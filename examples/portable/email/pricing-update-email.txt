PRO DID A LOT MORE IN 2026. HERE'S WHAT CHANGES IN 2027.

Hi there,

In 2026, Pro gained three new capabilities. Starting January 1, 2027, the Pro price moves from $49 to $59 per seat per month. As an existing customer, nothing changes until your next renewal after that date, and your first year at the new price is 10% off.


NEW IN PRO THIS YEAR

- AI ticket triage. New tickets are sorted, tagged and routed to the right person before anyone opens them.
- SLA reporting. See response and resolution times against your targets, by team and by queue.
- 24/7 chat support. Reach our support team by chat, any hour of any day.


WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOUR ACCOUNT

Until renewal:     You stay at $49 per seat per month.
First year after:  $53.10 per seat per month, starting at your first renewal
                   after January 1, 2027. That's the new price with a one-time
                   10% loyalty discount.
From year two:     $59 per seat per month, the standard Pro price.


EXAMPLE: A 15-SEAT PRO ACCOUNT

15 seats is about the average Pro account. Monthly totals:

  Today                      15 x $49      $735.00
  First year after renewal   15 x $53.10   $796.50  (with 10% loyalty discount)
  After that                 15 x $59      $885.00

That's $61.50 more per month in the first year, then $150 more per month after. Your own numbers depend on your seat count and renewal date.
