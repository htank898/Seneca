Why it works

Dishwashers don't scrub one plate at a time.

Robotics spent fifty years learning not to automate a job by copying how a person does it. AI at work is still building butlers: asking a model to drive PowerPoint and Word, tools made for a human with a mouse.

Seneca skips the butler. A browser is already a perfect presentation engine, print engine, and animation engine, and the model is fluent in its language. So every format here is code that renders: exact, editable, versionable, and portable to anyone with a browser.

Slide decks

A presentation as one self-contained HTML file: keyboard, click, and swipe navigation, transitions, step-by-step builds, speaker notes, and an overview grid. Exports to a one-slide-per-page PDF in one command. Any form factor: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 carousels, 4:3.

Stationery PDFs

ai-pdf

Pages laid out at real paper sizes with CSS @page. The stationery art is an SVG and CSS layer designed for the occasion, and Chromium's print engine writes a print-accurate PDF. Letters, invitations, itineraries, menus, certificates, one-pagers, proposals.

.pdfLetter / A4 / custombleed-safeprint-accurate

04

GIFs + sound

ai-gif

The animation is a pure function, renderFrame(t). A headless browser steps through every frame and ffmpeg encodes a seamless loop with an optimized palette. For the MP4, sound effects are synthesized in code with Web Audio and rendered offline: no audio files, no licenses.

.gif.mp4seamless loops14 sound presets1:1 · 16:9 · 9:16
Make a 4-second looping GIF of a progress bar that gets to 99% and starts over, for a Slack joke.
Fig. 04c · Live sound engine

Every sound is synthesized. Tap one.

This is the same engine the GIF skill uses to score MP4s, running in your browser right now. Cues are placed on the same timeline as renderFrame(t), so a whoosh lands exactly on the slide-in.

05

Narrated video

ai-video

A script split into scenes, a voice generated locally with Kokoro (open-source TTS), and an animation keyed to that voice. Outputs a portable MP4 (H.264 + AAC) with WebVTT and SRT captions. Characters can lip-sync from generated visemes. Everything runs on your machine.

.mp4.vtt.srtlocal TTSlip-syncno API keys
Turn this announcement into a 40-second narrated video for LinkedIn.
06

Scale you can feel

ai-scale

People can't feel “a trillion”. They can feel a stack of bills that passes the Space Station. A calculator does the math and writes every “taller than X, short of Y” caption; a prewritten engine animates a camera that zooms in powers of ten. One scene file becomes a short-form video, an interactive explorer, or slide stills.

How big is a trillion dollars? Make it a LinkedIn video.

Math

ai-math

Three routes, picked by what the math is for. Animation: Manim explainers in the 3Blue1Brown style, as MP4, GIF, or a transparent clip for other pieces. Typesetting: LaTeX rendered into any deck, guide, PDF, or GIF page with KaTeX at build time. Documents: real LaTeX papers compiled to PDF with Tectonic.

Finance viz

ai-finance-viz

A table of multiples is a butler: accurate, and nobody remembers it. Each metric gets a picture of what it means. P/E becomes years for $100 to pay you back, market cap and profit become a building and its rent, margins become a $100 bill getting trimmed. Numbers come from you or cited sources with dates, never from memory.

Under the hood

One pattern, every format

1 · Design passPalette, type, and one motif, chosen for the audience, reused across every piece in the package.
2 · Build in HTMLEach skill ships a template and a reference sheet of what a pro would think about: safe areas, paper sizes, loop timing, file budgets.
3 · RenderHeadless Chromium prints, screenshots, or steps frames. ffmpeg encodes. Kokoro speaks. Manim and Tectonic typeset.
4 · Check itselfEvery skill verifies its own output: no clipped text in any frame, honest scales, seamless loops, before handing you files.
Get it

Install Seneca in Claude Code

# as a plugin (recommended)
/plugin marketplace add https://github.com/htank898/Seneca.git
/plugin install seneca@seneca

# then just describe the situation
/seneca I have a meeting with the execs Thursday,
        summarize this migration plan for them

Requirements: Node 18+ and ffmpeg. Each skill installs its own Playwright on first render. Math animation adds Manim; narrated video adds Kokoro. Everything runs locally.

Or skip the router and name the format: “make a deck”, “make a GIF”, “how big is a billion”, “animate this proof”, “build a guide for our CRM”.
