# Revenue Operations Automation Lead — Lumen Desk

> Sample: Lumen Desk and this posting are fictional.

**Team:** Revenue · **Reports to:** Dana Whitfield, VP Revenue · **Location:** Remote (US)

Lumen Desk makes help-desk software for B2B teams. Inbound demand has grown faster than the systems that route it, and we want one person to own the automation that gets every lead to the right rep, fast, with a reason anyone can read.

## What you'll do
- Own lead routing and assignment in Salesforce end to end: rules, queues, round-robin, and exceptions.
- Build and maintain revenue automation in Salesforce Flow.
- Define, monitor, and report on speed-to-lead SLAs for every inbound source.
- Keep territories and account ownership clean as the sales team grows.
- Partner with Support to turn customer conversations into better routing and handoffs.

## What you bring
1. 5+ years in revenue operations or sales operations, with lead routing and assignment in Salesforce as a core part of the job.
2. Hands-on Salesforce Flow automation; Salesforce Administrator certification preferred.
3. A track record of improving speed-to-lead and SLA attainment, with numbers.
4. Experience designing territories and account ownership models.
5. Python or another scripting language for data integration and cleanup.
6. Experience administering Salesforce CPQ or another quoting tool.
7. Experience managing a team of 2+ operations specialists.
8. Bonus: background in customer support or help-desk software.
