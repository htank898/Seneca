Rationale

Pro is our most-used plan, and in 2026 it became a substantially bigger product. Three additions changed what a Pro seat delivers: AI ticket triage, which routes and prioritizes incoming tickets automatically; SLA reporting, which lets support leads show their own leadership how they are performing; and 24/7 chat support for Pro customers themselves.

The price has not moved with the product. At $49, Pro sits below every comparable tool, which range from $55 to $79 per seat. A move to $59 brings Pro into that range near its lower end, so we stay the better-value choice while charging for what customers now use.

There is also a cost reason. AI triage runs on paid model inference, and Pro’s gross margin fell from 81% to 74% after it launched. A price that reflects the feature lets us keep investing in it rather than rationing it.

Where $59 sits in the market

Comparable tools are priced from $55 to $79 per seat per month. Option A at $59 places Pro in the lower part of that range; Option B at $55 matches its floor; Option C keeps Pro $6 below it.

Customer communication plan

Every existing customer gets a personal notice that answers three questions in its first lines: what changes for them, when, and what it will cost. The notice leads with what Pro added in 2026, then shows their own seat count, their renewal date, their first-year price with the loyalty discount, and the price after that year. Sales and Support get the same facts as talk tracks and an FAQ before any notice goes out.

Risks and mitigations

RiskChurn rises more than the expected +0.4 pts, or stays high beyond 6 months.
MitigationThe first-year loyalty discount softens the step for every existing customer; churn is tracked monthly against the 2.0% assumption, and the what-if calculator shows how much headroom remains before the increase stops paying off.
RiskCustomers read the change as a price hike without new value.
MitigationEvery notice and talk track leads with AI triage, SLA reporting and 24/7 chat — the features they already use — before the number.
RiskSmaller, budget-bound accounts (for example, a 6-seat library) feel the change most.
MitigationPersonal notices show the exact monthly change ($294.00 to $318.60 for Fernhill), and later renewals get the most notice before their first bill moves.
RiskHolding (Option C) lets AI triage costs keep pressing on margin.
MitigationIf C is chosen, set the Q3 2027 revisit date now and track Pro margin from 74% until then.
