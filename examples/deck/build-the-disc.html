What robotics learned about automation, and what it means for how we use AI at work.

Open with the image: everyone has seen a robot vacuum slide under a couch. Nobody has seen a humanoid robot push an upright vacuum, and there's a reason for that.

Walk through each one. In every case the human tool had a shape that only made sense because a human was holding it. Remove the human and the shape goes too.

A vacuum has a handle because a person had to hold it.

Take the person away and the handle is just in the way.

Fig. 1, the butler

PowerPoint is a handle.

  • Built for a mouse. Drag a box, nudge a font, click through menus.
  • Familiar to viewers, so it became the default for every kind of message.
  • Awkward for AI. A complex file format that needs a special library, an MCP server, or Copilot.
Be fair to PowerPoint: it's familiar and anyone can make one. That's exactly why it's a human tool. Making AI drive it is building the butler.
