Sales Talk tracks and objections

What do I tell a customer who renews in February?

Nothing changes until their renewal date. They stay at $49 per seat through January, then renew at $53.10 per seat for their first year (the 10% loyalty discount), and $59 after that.

Harbor & Pine Dental Group is a good example: 12 seats, renewing February 14, 2027.

Say it like thisYour price holds until your February renewal. From then, you get our new Pro plan at a 10% loyalty rate for the full first year.
How do I explain why Pro is going up?

Lead with what the plan does now. In 2026 Pro gained three things customers use every day:

  • AI ticket triage, which routes and prioritizes tickets automatically
  • SLA reporting, so teams can show they hit their targets
  • 24/7 chat support

Comparable tools are priced at $55 to $79 per seat. At $59, Pro sits in the lower part of that range with more included.

Say it like thisPro does a lot more than it did a year ago, and the price now reflects that. It is still at the lower end of what comparable tools charge.
What does a new customer pay if they sign after January 1?

$59 per seat per month from January 1, 2027. The 10% loyalty discount is for existing customers only, so it does not apply to new accounts.

A prospect who signs before January 1 starts at $49 and becomes an existing customer. They move to the new price at their first renewal after that date, with the loyalty discount for that year.

What if leadership picks a different option?

Three options are on the table. Dana Whitfield, VP Revenue, decides by Friday, November 6, 2026.

  • A. $59 for everyone, with the first-year loyalty discount. Recommended by Finance and Product.
  • B. $55 for everyone, no discount.
  • C. Hold at $49 and revisit in Q3 2027.

Until the decision is announced, keep quoting current pricing and do not commit to a new price with customers.

Support What customers will ask, and what to say

A customer asks: when does my price change?

At their first renewal after January 1, 2027. Look up the renewal date on the account and give them that date. Before then, their price stays at $49 per seat.

Say it like thisYour price stays the same until your renewal on [date]. At that renewal you move to the new Pro price, with 10% off for the first year.
Is the loyalty discount taken off the old price or the new one?

The new one. It is 10% off $59, which is $53.10 per seat per month. It applies once, for the first year at the new price. After that year the price is $59.

It is not an additional 10% off today's $49.

Do customers lose or gain any features?

The proposal is a price change only. Pro customers keep everything on the plan, including the three additions from 2026: AI ticket triage, SLA reporting, and 24/7 chat support.

If a customer has not tried AI triage or SLA reporting yet, this is a good moment to show them.

A customer says they will cancel over the price. What do I say?

Acknowledge it, then make it concrete. Most concerns shrink once the customer sees their own numbers and dates.

  • Give their renewal date. Nothing changes before it.
  • Show their first-year cost at $53.10 per seat, not just $59.
  • Point to what Pro added in 2026 that they already use.

Finance The model and its assumptions

What is the revenue impact in year one?

About +$657K ARR in year one, from finance’s simple model at $59 with expected churn.

The $657K figure is finance’s estimate at $59 with the expected churn below. The $1,097,400 line is a ceiling: it ignores churn, the loyalty discount, and renewal timing.

What churn does the model assume?

Monthly logo churn on Pro is 1.6% today. The model adds 0.4 points for the first six months after the change, to 2.0%, then returns to 1.6%.

What happens to accounts that churn?

They are already counted. The +$657K estimate is net of the extra churn, so the remaining accounts paying more outweigh the revenue lost from accounts that leave.

For scale: 0.4 points of 1,240 accounts is roughly 5 extra accounts a month, or about 30 over the six-month window (simple estimate, not compounded). At the average of 15 seats, each one is about $735 a month at today’s price.

Why change pricing now?

Gross margin on Pro fell from 81% to 74% after AI triage was added, because of model costs. The new price funds those features at a healthy margin.

Pro is also the most-used plan and is priced below comparable tools ($55 to $79), so the change brings it in line with the market.
